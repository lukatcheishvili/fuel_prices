Loyalty cards

How card prices are calculated

With "Loyalty card prices" switched on, the dashboard shows pump price minus your card's discount. Set your level per company in Settings (the default is the entry level a new cardholder gets). Bigger discounts need more fuel or spend per month. Discounts as published on each company's own site, checked 03 Oct 2026.
